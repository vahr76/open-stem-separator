- your own recordings;

- content whose owner gave you permission;
- backups or study copies allowed by the laws and terms that apply to you.

The project is designed for music study, production workflows, personal analysis, and future stem-separation workflows on lawful source material. Users are responsible for complying with copyright law, platform terms, and local rules.
